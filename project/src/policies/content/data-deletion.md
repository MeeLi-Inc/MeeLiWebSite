Data & Account Deletion (MeeLi)

Effective June 1, 2026 — Version 1.0 
Account Deletion & Your Data
MeeLi is built on the idea that your data belongs to you.
If you decide to leave, you should be able to do so clearly, simply, and with confidence.

How to Delete Your Account
You can delete your account at any time directly in the app:
Profile → Settings → Delete Account

What Happens When You Delete
When you delete your account:
	•	your profile becomes inactive immediately
	•	your content is no longer visible to other users
	•	your account enters a 14-day recovery period

Recovery Period (14 Days)
During this time:
	•	your data is not visible to others
	•	you can restore your account by logging back in
After 14 days, your account and associated data are permanently deleted.

What Gets Deleted
After the recovery period, MeeLi permanently deletes your:
	•	profile information
	•	posts, photos, and videos
	•	messages and chats
	•	Journey data (including journal entries, memories, and timeline activity)
	•	Account credentials and login information (email address or phone number) 

What May Be Retained
To maintain safety and improve the platform, MeeLi may retain limited internal records related to:
	•	content moderation and review decisions
	•	platform integrity and security
This information:
	•	is stored in a limited and internal form
	•	is not used to identify you as an active user
	•	is never sold or shared externally

Important Notes
	•	Account deletion is permanent after the 14-day recovery period
	•	Deleted accounts cannot be restored after this period
	•	Creating a new account does not restore previous data
For full details on how your data is handled, see MeeLi's Privacy Policy.

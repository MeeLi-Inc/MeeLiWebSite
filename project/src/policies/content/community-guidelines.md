## **🌍 1\. What MeeLi Is**

MeeLi is a space to **meet life as it really is**—through people, stories, and genuine experiences.

This is not a platform for performance, perfection, or noise.  
 It’s a place for:

* real moments  
* honest expression  
* meaningful connection

We’re building something different—and that means we all share responsibility for keeping it that way. Our guidelines apply to *all* users regardless of age, device, or access method.

---

## **🤝 2\. How We Treat Each Other**

We expect everyone on MeeLi to show up with basic respect.

### **Allowed:**

* Honest opinions and experiences  
* Vulnerability and personal stories  
* Healthy disagreement (without attacking the person)

### **Not allowed:**

* Harassment, bullying, or intimidation  
* Hate speech or discrimination (based on race, ethnicity, national origin, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, religion, disability, age)  
* Targeted attacks or encouraging harm toward others

👉 **Simple rule:**  
 If it makes someone feel unsafe being human here, it doesn’t belong.

---

## **🎥 3\. What You Share**

MeeLi is built for **real life—not recycled content**.

### **We encourage:**

* Your own stories, experiences, and perspectives  
* Everyday moments (big or small)  
* Growth, health, and self-discovery

### **Not allowed:**

* Reposting movies, TV clips, or copyrighted content you don’t own  
* Low-effort, mass-produced, or spammy content  
* Content created purely to chase attention without substance

👉 This isn’t about perfection—it’s about **authenticity**.

By posting on MeeLi, you confirm you own or have rights to the content you share. 

---

## **🤖 4\. AI & Content Authenticity**

MeeLi exists because real human stories matter. AI can be a useful tool — but it can't live your life for you, and it doesn't belong here as a substitute for genuine expression. 

**Allowed:**

* Using AI-powered editing tools to enhance your content (captions, trimming, noise reduction, translation)  
* Using AI to help you *organize or refine* thoughts you then express in your own words and voice

**Not allowed:**

* AI-generated photos, videos, or written posts presented as your real experience  
* Deepfakes or AI-generated images, audio, or video depicting real people without their clear consent  
* Avatars, personas, or profiles that are AI-generated or not representative of a real person  
* Automated or bot-driven posting of any kind  
* Mass-produced, low-effort content designed to flood the platform rather than connect with people

👉 *The test is simple: did a real person live it, feel it, or think it? If the answer is no — it doesn't belong on MeeLi.*

---

## **🧠 5\. Sensitive Topics & Real Life**

MeeLi was built for the full human experience — not just the highlight reel. Our Journey feature exists precisely because growth isn't linear, and some of the most meaningful connections happen when someone shares something real and hard. That kind of honesty is welcome here.

**Allowed:**

* Sharing personal struggles, setbacks, and difficult emotions  
* Mental health conversations — including anxiety, depression, grief, and burnout  
* Documenting your healing, recovery, or growth journey  
* Discussing difficult life experiences in a way that's honest and human

**Not allowed:**

* Content that promotes, glorifies, or provides methods for self-harm or suicide  
* Graphic depictions of injury, illness, or death without clear personal or educational context  
* Content that promotes eating disorders, extreme dieting, or dangerous fitness behaviors  
* Encouraging others toward harmful, dangerous, or illegal behavior  
* Content that is gratuitously triggering with no redemptive or connective purpose

**If you're struggling:** MeeLi will surface crisis resources contextually when certain topics are detected. You can also reach the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline anytime by calling or texting **988**, or text HOME to **741741** for the Crisis Text Line.

👉 *We make space for real life — including the hard parts. What we don't make space for is content that puts you or someone else at risk.*

---

## **🌏 6\. What We Don’t Focus On**

MeeLi is intentionally different. Other platforms are built around news cycles, outrage, and opinion wars. We're not. This space is protected — and that means some content that's perfectly normal elsewhere simply doesn't belong here.

**Not allowed:**

* Political content of any kind — including personal opinions, commentary, campaigning, propaganda, or content tied to political figures or movements  
* War content, graphic global conflict, or news-driven coverage of world events  
* Content that exists primarily to shock, enrage, or divide — including rage-bait, inflammatory hot takes designed to generate conflict, and content that recycles divisive news or cultural moments without any genuine personal connection to the topic  
* Celebrity gossip, viral drama, or content centered on public figures rather than your own life and experience

**The standard that runs through all of this:** If the content isn't rooted in *your* real life and *your* genuine experience, it probably doesn't belong on MeeLi — regardless of the topic.

👉 *This isn't about ignoring the world. It's about building something the world doesn't already have.*

## **🛑 7\. Safety First**

MeeLi holds a higher standard than most platforms — intentionally. The following violations are taken seriously, acted on immediately, and in some cases reported to authorities. There is no grace period for content or behavior that puts people at risk.

**Tier 1 — Zero Tolerance (Immediate Permanent Ban \+ Reported to Authorities)**

These violations result in immediate and permanent account termination. MeeLi will report to the relevant authorities and/or the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) as required by law. There is no appeal process for Tier 1 violations.

* Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) or any sexual content involving minors  
* Credible threats of violence toward any individual or group  
* Content that facilitates, promotes, or coordinates real-world violence or terrorism  
* Non-consensual sharing of intimate images (deepfakes or real)


**Tier 2 — Severe (Immediate Permanent Ban)**

These violations result in immediate and permanent account termination. Users may submit an appeal within 30 days.

* Hate speech or content that dehumanizes individuals or groups based on race, ethnicity, national origin, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, religion, disability, or age  
* Sexual exploitation or coercion of any kind  
* Doxxing — sharing someone's private personal information without consent  
* Impersonation of another real person with intent to deceive or harm  
* Coordinated inauthentic behavior — fake accounts, bot networks, or organized manipulation


**Tier 3 — Serious (Escalating Consequences)**

These violations result in content removal and account restriction on first offense, escalating to permanent ban for repeat or severe occurrences. Users may appeal within 30 days.

* Harassment, bullying, or targeted intimidation  
* Scams, fraud, or deceptive practices  
* Repeated violations of any Community Guidelines  
* Content that repeatedly conflicts with the spirit of MeeLi despite prior warnings  
* Promotion of self-harm, eating disorders, or dangerous behaviors  
* AI-generated content presented as real personal experience


**Law Enforcement Cooperation**

MeeLi will cooperate fully with law enforcement investigations when legally required or when we determine that cooperation is necessary to protect the safety of our users or the public.

**Appeals**

Users who believe a Tier 2 or Tier 3 enforcement action was made in error may submit an appeal by contacting MeeLi support within 30 days of the action. Appeals for Tier 1 violations will not be reviewed under any circumstances.

**Our Commitment & Limitations**

MeeLi reviews reports as promptly as possible and prioritizes them by severity. However, we cannot guarantee that all reported content will be reviewed or removed within a specific timeframe, and we are not liable for content that remains on the platform while under review or that goes unreported. By using MeeLi, you acknowledge that:

* MeeLi is not obligated to monitor all content proactively  
* Reporting a violation does not guarantee a specific outcome or timeline  
* MeeLi's failure to act on any particular piece of content does not waive our right to act on similar content in the future  
* MeeLi makes moderation decisions at its sole discretion and reserves the right to err on the side of community safety

We will always prioritize the safety of our community — but we are a platform, not a guarantor of perfect moderation.

👉 *We're building a safer space — not just a different one. If you see something that doesn't belong here, please report it. We take every report seriously.*

*To report a violation, use the in-app report button or contact us at support@meeli.social.*

## **💫 8\. Faith, Spirituality & Personal Beliefs**

MeeLi is a place for the full human journey — and for many people, faith, spirituality, and personal beliefs are a meaningful part of that. Whether you're rooted in an organized religion, a spiritual practice, a personal philosophy, or still figuring it out — that journey is welcome here.

**Allowed:**

* Sharing your personal faith, spiritual practice, or belief system  
* Documenting how your beliefs shape your life, growth, and decisions  
* Respectful conversation and curiosity about different worldviews  
* Cultural and ancestral traditions that are meaningful to you

**Not allowed:**

* Using your beliefs as a basis to judge, demean, or exclude others  
* Proselytizing — pressuring or repeatedly urging others to adopt your beliefs  
* Content that frames any group of people as inferior based on their faith or lack thereof  
* Hate speech or discrimination tied to religion or belief system

👉 *Your journey with faith and meaning belongs here. What doesn't belong is using belief as a weapon against someone else's humanity.*

Violations involving hate or discrimination based on belief are subject to enforcement under Section 7\.

## **📢 9\. Ads, Promotion & Spam**

MeeLi is not an advertising platform — and we mean that in every sense. This space is built for real people and real connection, not commercial gain.

Not allowed:

* Spam of any kind  
* Self-promotion, brand promotion, or advertising of products or services  
* Affiliate links or referral codes of any kind  
* Undisclosed or disclosed paid partnerships or brand deals  
* Fake engagement tactics — buying followers, likes, or manufactured interaction  
* Any commercial arrangement between a user and an outside brand conducted on or through MeeLi

👉 If it’s selling something — including yourself as a brand — it doesn’t belong here.

## **⚖️ 10\. Enforcement & Judgment**

Not everything fits neatly into rules — and we won’t pretend otherwise. MeeLi is a living community, and our approach to enforcement reflects that.

How we make decisions:

When content or behavior falls into a gray area, we evaluate based on three things:

* Context — what’s the full picture?  
* Intent — what was the purpose behind it?  
* Impact — how did it affect the community?

What we may act on:

Beyond the specific violations outlined in these guidelines, MeeLi reserves the right to take action on content or accounts that:

* Violate the spirit of MeeLi even if not explicitly covered by a specific rule  
* Create a negative, unsafe, or inauthentic environment for other users  
* Represent a pattern of behavior that undermines community trust, even if individual actions seem minor  
* Conflict with MeeLi’s core values of authenticity, health, connection, and growth

Our commitment:

Our goal is never punishment for its own sake — it’s protecting the people and the space we’re building together. We approach every enforcement decision with context, care, and consistency.

👉 The rules exist to protect the community. So does our judgment when the rules run out. For more details, refer to our **Safety & Moderation Policy**.

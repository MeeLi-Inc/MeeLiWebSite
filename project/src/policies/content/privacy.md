6.  Data Storage & Security
MeeLi stores and processes data using secure infrastructure in the United States. Your data is hosted on Amazon Web Services (AWS) in the United States, including the us-east-1 region in Northern Virginia, with primary application data stored on MongoDB Atlas. Both providers meet industry standards for security and reliability.
AWS supports cloud hosting, file storage through Amazon S3, email delivery through Amazon SES, account authentication through Amazon Cognito, background processing, and content delivery infrastructure. MongoDB Atlas stores primary application data, including accounts, posts, and Journey content.
We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure.

Sensitive Data
Some information on MeeLi—such as your journal entries, memories, and personal content—may be more sensitive.

7.  Data Retention & Deletion
While your account is active
MeeLi retains your account data, posts, Journey entries, and activity data for as long as your account is active.
You can delete individual content—such as posts, memories, or entries—at any time within the app.

Account Deletion
You can delete your MeeLi account at any time within the app.
When you delete your account:
	•	your account is deactivated
	•	your data is retained for a limited period in case you choose to return
	•	after this period, your data is permanently deleted from our systems

Recovery Period
MeeLi provides a 14-day recovery period after account deletion.
During this time:
	•	your data is not visible to others
	•	you may restore your account by logging back in
After the recovery period, your data is permanently deleted.

Retention for Safety & Improvement
In some cases, MeeLi may retain limited internal records related to content moderation and platform safety.
This may include information about how content is reviewed or handled, even after content is removed or accounts are deleted.
This data is:
	•	used only to improve moderation processes and platform safety
	•	stored in a limited and internal form
	•	not used for advertising or shared externally

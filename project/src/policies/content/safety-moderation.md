# **🛡️ MeeLi Safety & Moderation Policy**

Effective June 11, 2026 — Version 0.3.0  
---

## **⚡ Moderation Overview**

**Community sets the tone → Community reporting → Human-first review → Action if needed**

* **Community Sets the Tone**  
   Clear guidelines and intentional culture shape what belongs on MeeLi.  
* **Community Reporting**  
   Users flag content that feels unsafe or violates the rules.  
* **Human-First Review**  
   Our team reviews flagged content with context and care.  
* **Action (If Needed)**  
   We will take appropriate action, which may include consequences applied according to MeeLi's three-tier enforcement system, ranging from content removal to permanent account termination 

---

## **🌱 1\. Our Approach**

MeeLi is built to be a **safe, real, and human space**.

We combine:

* clear community guidelines  
* user reporting  
* human review

to ensure the platform stays aligned with its values.

---

## **🔍 2\. How Moderation Works (Today)**

At this stage, moderation is **human-first**.

### **Step 1: Community Reporting**

If something feels off, unsafe, or against the guidelines:  
 👉 users can report content directly in the app

---

### **Step 2: Internal Review**

Our team reviews flagged content by looking at:

* the content itself  
* the context  
* the intent  
* the potential impact

---

### **Step 3: Action**

Based on the review, we may:

* remove content  
* issue a warning  
* restrict account activity  
* suspend or permanently ban the account

Actions are applied in accordance with MeeLi's three-tier enforcement system as defined in the Community & Content Guidelines. When in doubt, refer to the tier definitions before taking action. 

---

## **⚖️ 3\. How We Make Decisions**

Not everything is black and white.

We consider:

* **Context** — What’s actually happening?  
* **Intent** — Was harm intended?  
* **Impact** — Could this hurt someone or the community?

👉 This allows us to handle **gray areas fairly**, not just rigidly.

We aim to review reports as quickly as possible, prioritizing content that may cause harm.

---

## **🚫 4\. Enforcement Tiers** 

MeeLi applies a three-tier enforcement system. All moderation actions must correspond to the appropriate tier. When in doubt, escalate to a senior team member before acting.

**Tier 1 — Zero Tolerance** *Immediate permanent ban \+ report to authorities. No appeal. No exceptions.*

* Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) or any sexual content involving minors — report immediately to NCMEC via CyberTipline  
* Credible threats of violence toward any individual or group  
* Content that facilitates, promotes, or coordinates real-world violence or terrorism  
* Non-consensual sharing of intimate images (deepfakes or real)

**Tier 1 Protocol:** Any team member who encounters suspected CSAM must escalate immediately to \[designated senior contact\]. Do not attempt to review, save, or share the content beyond what is necessary for reporting. File a CyberTipline report at missingkids.org/gethelpnow/cybertipline within 24 hours of discovery. 

**Tier 2 — Severe** *Immediate permanent ban. User may appeal within 30 days.*

* Hate speech or content that dehumanizes individuals or groups based on race, ethnicity, national origin, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, religion, disability, or age  
* Sexual exploitation or coercion of any kind  
* Doxxing — sharing someone's private personal information without consent  
* Impersonation of another real person with intent to deceive or harm  
* Coordinated inauthentic behavior — fake accounts, bot networks, or organized manipulation

**Tier 2 Protocol:** Remove content immediately. Issue permanent ban. Document decision with reasoning. Inform user of ban and 30-day appeal window via in-app notification.

**Tier 3 — Serious** *Content removal \+ account restriction on first offense. Escalates to permanent ban for repeat or severe violations. User may appeal within 30 days.*

* Harassment, bullying, or targeted intimidation  
* Scams, fraud, or deceptive practices  
* Repeated Community Guidelines violations  
* Content that repeatedly conflicts with the spirit of MeeLi despite prior warnings  
* Promotion of self-harm, eating disorders, or dangerous behaviors  
* AI-generated content presented as real personal experience

**Tier 3 Protocol:** Remove content. Issue first warning and account restriction. Document pattern of behavior. Escalate to permanent ban if violation is severe or repeated.

All enforcement actions must be logged with: date and time, content description, tier applied, action taken, and team member who made the decision. This record is confidential and internal only.

## **⚠️ 5\. Gray Areas**

Some content requires careful judgment, including:

* emotionally charged personal content  
* sensitive real-world topics  
* humor or sarcasm that could be misunderstood

👉 These are reviewed **case-by-case** using context, intent, and impact.

### 

### **🤝 Shared Review Process**

For gray-area decisions, we don’t rely on a single perspective.

When needed, content may be reviewed collaboratively by members of the MeeLi team and trained ambassadors to ensure decisions are:

* fair  
* balanced  
* aligned with MeeLi’s values

👉 This helps prevent one-sided decisions and keeps moderation grounded in the community we’re building.

**📬 8\. Appeals Process**

Users may appeal Tier 2 and Tier 3 enforcement decisions within 30 days by contacting MeeLi support.

**When an appeal is received:**

* Acknowledge receipt within 48 hours  
* Assign to a senior team member not involved in the original decision  
* Review original decision, context, and any new information provided  
* Respond with a final decision within 14 days  
* Document outcome

**Possible outcomes:**

* Decision upheld — ban or restriction remains  
* Decision reversed — account reinstated, content restored if appropriate  
* Decision modified — restriction reduced or adjusted

Tier 1 appeals are never reviewed. If a user contacts support about a Tier 1 ban, acknowledge receipt and confirm the decision is final. Do not engage further or re-review the case under any circumstances.

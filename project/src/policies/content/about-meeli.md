Welcome to

**MeeLi \- Meet Life**

*A healthy social app for students.*

*Effective June 11, 2026 — Version 0.3.0*

---

## **Our Mission**

### **Bringing Soul Back to Social Media**

**Life isn’t meant to be watched. It’s meant to be lived.**

**MeeLi is built on a simple belief:**  
 **the best moments in life happen offline.**

**We exist to help people create real connections, explore who they are,**  
 **and experience life as it actually is.**

**Not through followers.**  
 **Not through likes.**  
 **But through real moments and real people.**

---

## **What MeeLi Is**

**MeeLi isn’t about content.**  
 **It’s about life.**

**It’s a space to:**

* **share your real experiences**  
* **connect with people who genuinely matter**  
* **explore your interests, passions, and identity**

**Every part of MeeLi is designed to move you closer to life — not away from it.**
